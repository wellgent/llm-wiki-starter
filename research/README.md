# Research

A research record is the evidence from one research ticket: what was asked, how it was investigated and what was found.
The answer goes on the ticket, and a decision that outlives the map goes onto the wiki's pages.
The record is what stands behind them.

## Naming

- `research/r-YYYYMMDD-<slug>.md`, dated by the run, with a lowercase hyphenated slug.
- The record lands on `main`, and the ticket's resolution comment links it.

## Immutable

A record is never edited once written.
A repeat run on the same question is a new record with a new date, and the old record stays.
A new investigation goes back to primary sources: a prior record supplies leads, not conclusions.

## Shape

1. An H1 title.
2. A one-paragraph lede: what was asked and what was found.
3. `## Question` - the precise question and its scope.
4. `## Method` - what was read or run.
5. `## Findings` - each finding with its source inline. Doubt is stated in plain words.
6. `## Recommended wiki updates` - the pages to change and how, or `None.`
